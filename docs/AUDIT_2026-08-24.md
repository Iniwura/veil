## Executive summary

The winner build materially changes the trust, liveness and privacy model of the earlier VEIL prototype.

The largest protocol improvements are:

1. Draw timing is enforced by the contract.
2. Draw close, BlindDraw execution and KMS-proof finalization are permissionless.
3. A late caller cannot retroactively alter an expired round with a late deposit or withdrawal.
4. Saving no longer fails solely because the bounded 32-seat FHE draw roster is full.
5. Users can decrypt their own position history and exact personal draw odds without exposing peer weights.
6. Yield is attached to a predetermined sequential round cursor rather than a caller-selected round.
7. A keeper cannot allocate an unsealed strategy bucket.
8. Prize delivery is permissionless but the recipient is fixed to the proof-finalized winner.
9. The winner can decrypt the awarded amount after automatic delivery.
10. The Sepolia asset path uses Zama's published cUSDC mock wrapper by default rather than a project-specific token.

The remaining material limitations are competition-scale constraints and production integration work, not hidden claims of production readiness.

## Findings status

- **U-01 — Privileged draw close — Fixed.** `closeDraw()` is permissionless and timestamp-gated.
- **U-02 — Privileged BlindDraw — Fixed.** `blindDraw(roundId)` is permissionless after snapshot.
- **U-03 — Winner finalization liveness — Fixed.** Any caller may submit the valid Zama KMS decryption proof.
- **U-04 — Late transaction changes expired round — Fixed.** State-changing saver actions roll an expired period before mutating live balances. Snapshot eligibility is evaluated at the scheduled cutoff.
- **U-05 — Saving blocked by full draw roster — Fixed.** A saver can retain a confidential position even if no draw seat is available.
- **U-06 — Permanent stale roster occupancy — Mitigated.** Seats expire after 30 days and can be pruned. The 32-seat cap remains.
- **U-07 — Zero-weight round stuck — Fixed.** A KMS-proven zero winner moves the round to `CANCELLED`.
- **U-08 — User cannot see own private activity — Fixed.** Position state includes encrypted balance, cumulative deposit/withdrawal and latest activity handles with owner ACL.
- **U-09 — User cannot know exact personal odds — Fixed.** A round participant may decrypt their own frozen weight and encrypted snapshot total, then calculate odds locally. Peer weights remain private.
- **U-10 — Keeper chooses yield destination — Fixed.** Yield can only fund `yieldRoundId`, and the cursor advances sequentially.
- **U-11 — Keeper races unfinished yield sync — Fixed.** Allocation requires the strategy to seal the encrypted bucket first.
- **U-12 — Strategy controls draw/winner — Fixed.** Strategy authority is isolated to realized-yield accrual and sealing.
- **U-13 — Winner must manually claim — Fixed.** `deliverPrize(roundId)` is permissionless and always transfers to the finalized winner.
- **U-14 — Prize amount lost after delivery — Fixed.** `awardedAmount` preserves the encrypted actual transfer for winner-only decryption.
- **U-15 — Keeper can redirect payout — Fixed.** Prize recipient is read from the finalized pool winner. The caller receives nothing.
- **U-16 — Cancelled round strands yield — Fixed.** Sealed encrypted yield can be carried to the next sequential round.
- **U-17 — Old VEIL deployment accidentally used by new UI — Fixed pre-deploy.** Old defaults were removed. Protocol addresses remain explicitly pending until the fresh UNVEIL deployment is committed.
- **U-18 — Live market yield on Sepolia — Open by design.** Sepolia proves asset-backed confidential yield accounting with a controlled strategy operator. It does not pretend to earn Morpho mainnet yield.
- **U-19 — 32-participant FHE draw cap — Open.** This is a competition-scale implementation. Production scaling requires a different eligibility/aggregation architecture.
- **U-20 — Temporary seat/Sybil pressure — Open.** Expiry and pruning limit permanent blockage, but economic anti-spam design is still required.
- **U-21 — Encrypted aggregate arithmetic bounds — Open.** Production use requires explicit maximum-deposit/AUM bounds and overflow analysis for `euint64` aggregate arithmetic.
- **U-22 — Metadata privacy — Accepted boundary.** Wallet addresses, transaction timing/occurrence, draw membership and final winner remain public.

## Draw-lifecycle review

### Timing

`drawPeriod` and `nextDrawClosesAt` are contract state. A caller cannot close early.

When a call arrives after a deadline, the protocol advances from the scheduled boundary rather than from the caller's chosen timestamp. If several periods elapsed, the next deadline is rolled forward into the future.

### Cutoff integrity

A saver action checks for an expired round before changing the live encrypted position. Snapshot eligibility is evaluated against the scheduled close time. This prevents a transaction after the deadline from changing the expired round merely because a keeper was late.

### Permissionless maintenance

After the time condition is satisfied:

- any caller may close/snapshot
- any caller may execute BlindDraw
- any caller may submit a valid KMS public-decryption proof
- any caller may route a sealed, predetermined yield bucket
- any caller may deliver the encrypted prize to the finalized winner

The controlled Sepolia strategy operator is deliberately not a draw administrator. Its only long-lived role is moving and sealing realized confidential strategy assets.

## Private-user review

### Position

The current position tracks encrypted:

- balance
- total deposited
- total withdrawn
- last deposit
- last withdrawal

Handles are contract-authorized and owner-authorized. The browser requests explicit wallet-scoped user decryption and displays plaintext only in the user's session.

### Exact odds

For a frozen round, each participant gets ACL access to:

- their own encrypted weight
- the round's encrypted total weight

The frontend decrypts those two values for the participant and computes odds locally. A participant does not receive another user's individual draw weight.

### Withdrawals

Withdrawals preserve ERC-7984-style full-request-or-zero behavior. An oversized confidential request does not publicly reveal an insufficient-balance condition and cannot spend another depositor's pooled custody.

## Prize review

`VeilPrizeVault` keeps prize assets separate from principal custody.

A permissionless caller may deliver a funded prize, but:

- the destination is the proof-finalized winner
- the caller cannot redirect the recipient
- the winner's principal accounting is not changed
- the actual encrypted transferred amount is preserved for the winner to decrypt later

The product UX describes this as delivery rather than requiring the user to understand a claim function.

## Yield review

The Sepolia competition architecture separates three concerns:

1. The strategy operator realizes and seals confidential testnet yield.
2. A permissionless keeper routes an already sealed sequential bucket.
3. The pool independently proves the winner.

This prevents a keeper from choosing the round and prevents the strategy role from choosing the winner.

The competition flow remains a simulation of realized yield using actual confidential test assets. It is not a claim that the user principal is currently deployed into the mainnet Steakhouse/Morpho strategy.

## Product/UI review

The winner build fixes the earlier single-page anchor architecture.

Dedicated routes now cover:

- overview
- save/withdraw
- draw lifecycle
- private vault
- prizes
- history
- protocol/security

The landing page now contains:

- direct product outcome
- interactive encryption demonstration
- live draw/proof rail
- problem framing
- end-to-end product flow
- PoolTogether/public-prize-pool privacy comparison
- security/privacy boundary
- FAQ
- repeated CTA

First-run guided onboarding can be skipped and replayed.

The motion system is built around ciphertext resolution, sealing, proof state and weighted FHE selection. Blackout is treated as the quality floor rather than the target. `prefers-reduced-motion` is supported.

## Conclusion

The winner build removes the largest trust and UX gaps in the earlier VEIL prototype: manual draw administration, opaque owner-only private UX, manual winner claims, caller-directed yield allocation, and thin single-page product structure.

The remaining release work is evidence and deployment work plus clearly documented production limitations. The project should remain a draft until that evidence is complete.
