## Executive summary

UNVEIL materially changes the trust, liveness and privacy model of the earlier VEIL prototype.

The largest improvements are:

1. The contract enforces draw timing.
2. Draw close is permissionless.
3. BlindDraw execution is permissionless.
4. KMS winner-proof finalization is permissionless.
5. Late saver actions cannot change an expired round.
6. A full draw roster no longer blocks confidential saving.
7. Users can privately inspect their own position history.
8. Users can privately calculate exact personal draw odds.
9. Yield follows a predetermined sequential round cursor.
10. A keeper cannot allocate an unsealed strategy bucket.
11. Prize delivery is permissionless and fixed to the proved winner.
12. The winner can decrypt the awarded amount after delivery.

The remaining limitations are documented competition-scale and production-integration work.

## Draw lifecycle

### Timing

`drawPeriod` and `nextDrawClosesAt` are contract state. A caller cannot close early.

Late calls advance from the scheduled boundary rather than from a caller-selected timestamp.

### Cutoff integrity

A saver action checks for an expired round before changing the live encrypted position.

Snapshot eligibility is evaluated against the scheduled close time.

### Permissionless maintenance

After the required state or time condition is met, any caller may:

- close and snapshot
- execute BlindDraw
- submit a valid KMS public-decryption proof
- route a sealed predetermined yield bucket
- deliver the encrypted prize to the finalized winner

The Sepolia strategy operator is not a draw administrator.

Its controlled role is moving and sealing realized confidential strategy assets.

## Private-user review

### Position

The current position tracks encrypted:

- balance
- total deposited
- total withdrawn
- last deposit
- last withdrawal

The connected owner can explicitly decrypt those handles for the current browser session.

### Exact odds

For a frozen round, a participant has ACL access to:

- their own encrypted weight
- the round's encrypted total weight

The browser decrypts those two values and calculates exact odds locally.

A participant does not receive another user's individual draw weight.

### Withdrawals

Withdrawals preserve ERC-7984-style full-request-or-zero behavior.

An oversized request does not publish an insufficient-balance result.

It also cannot spend another participant's pooled custody.

## Prize review

`VeilPrizeVault` keeps prize assets separate from principal custody.

A permissionless caller may deliver a funded prize, but the recipient is the proved winner.

The caller cannot redirect the payout or receive it.

Winner principal accounting is not changed by prize delivery.

The actual encrypted transfer is preserved for winner-only decryption after delivery.

## Yield review

The Sepolia competition flow separates three roles:

1. The strategy operator realizes and seals confidential testnet yield.
2. A permissionless keeper routes the sealed sequential bucket.
3. The pool independently proves the winner.

The Sepolia flow does not claim that principal currently earns live Morpho mainnet yield.

The production path is documented in `PRODUCTION_YIELD_PATH.md`.

## Product and UI review

The winner build replaces the earlier single-page anchor architecture with dedicated routes.

Routes cover:

- overview
- save and withdraw
- draw lifecycle
- private vault
- prizes
- history
- protocol and security

The landing page now includes:

- direct product outcome
- interactive encryption demonstration
- live draw and proof rail
- problem framing
- end-to-end product flow
- privacy comparison
- security boundary
- FAQ
- repeated CTA

First-run onboarding can be skipped and replayed.

The motion language is built around ciphertext, sealing, weighted FHE selection and proof finality.

Blackout is the quality floor rather than the target.

`prefers-reduced-motion` is supported.
